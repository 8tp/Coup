# Spanish (es) glossary

One catalog for Spain and Latin America. Character and action names follow the
Spanish edition of Coup published by Zacatrus ("Coup: Ciudad Corrupta", 2013;
now sold as "Coup: Edición Clásica" and "Coup + Reforma"), checked against its
rulebook. Unreviewed by a native speaker.

## Register and conventions

- **Tú** throughout, singular, never "vosotros" or "ustedes" (no line needs a
  plural "you"). Tú is the norm for games in Spain and most of Latin America;
  River Plate players use vos but read tú without friction.
- **Neutral vocabulary.** Avoided Spain-only or country-specific words:
  "coger" (vulgar in parts of Latin America), "móvil" vs "celular" (body
  text says "teléfono"; one FAQ question names both because people search
  both), "ordenador" (used "computadora"; "máquina" for computer players),
  "ir a por", "pillar", "colar", leísmo ("le obligas"). Present tense where
  possible; where a past is needed, the simple past ("ganaste"), which reads
  naturally everywhere.
- **Unknown gender.** Lines about a named player avoid gendered adjectives:
  "queda fuera", "farol descubierto", "Victoria: {name}", "fuera antes que
  nadie", "la segunda persona en quedar fuera". Generic statements about
  "un jugador" use the generic masculine, as Spanish rules do.
- **The reader as a player param.** `log.you` / `gameover.you` are "ti" and
  appear only after a preposition ("contra ti"); everywhere else the line has
  a `you` branch so the verb or clitic changes ("te desafía", "Desafías").
- **Articles.** Named glossary params in rules and tutorial take the article
  their noun needs (el Duque, la Condesa, la Extorsión, la Ayuda exterior,
  la Reserva, el Coup). Generic `{character}` / `{action}` params never do.
- **Plurals.** one / other. CLDR also has `many` for exact multiples of a
  million ("1 millón de monedas"); no count in this game can reach it, so
  it is not written and falls back to `other`.
- Seconds are written "45s" (no space) because the timer's figure face
  wraps or spaces "45 s" badly.
