# On-screen copy

Scope: anything a player reads. The tutorial, the coach, prompts, buttons,
hints, the rules modal, toasts. Code comments are out of scope.

Write the sentence you would say to a friend across the table, then cut it.
Adapted from the Aimperion house style (`Aimperion/docs/voice.md`), which
cites its sources.

## Cut these

- Em dashes joining clauses. Use a full stop, a comma or a colon. An empty
  table cell can still show a dash.
- "Not X, but Y" and "not just X, it's Y". Say Y.
- Slogans and aphorisms as headings: "Keep a card. Outlast the court.",
  "Same bluffing, new politics". A heading states the rule.
- Rhetorical questions ("Bluff or not?", "Is Alex lying?"). Say what the
  player can do.
- Exclamation marks, bold for emphasis, CAPITALS for emphasis.
- Eyebrow labels over every heading ("2 of 6 · Your turn"). The progress
  rail already says where you are.
- A line that repeats the heading, the button, or what the board shows.
- Metaphors in place of the mechanic ("a Duke claim turned inside out",
  "shake up their hand", "start a story").

## Do this

- One idea per sentence. Contractions are fine: "you're out", "don't".
- Real numbers: "7 coins", "draw 2, put back 2".
- The game's own nouns, every time: card, influence, claim, challenge,
  block, Coup, coins, reserve, faction, Loyalist, Reformist. "Influence"
  is introduced once as the name for your face-down cards; after that the
  tutorial and coach say "lose a card".
- Buttons are verb phrases of one to three words.
- A tutorial note is one line of feedback on what just happened.
- Keep every fact the old copy had.
